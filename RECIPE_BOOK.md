### 1. Crispy Quinoa Granola
![Crispy Quinoa Granola](assets/recipes/imported/crispy-quinoa-granola.png)

**Recipe Owner:**  
**Source:** Noom cookbook (`IMG_5292` and `IMG_5293`)  
**Date Made:**  
**Rating:**  
**Would Make Again:**  
**Prep time:** 10 min  
**Cook time:** 25 min  
**Estimated macros:** ~241 cal | 7.3g protein | 11.4g fat | 31.5g carbs (granola only, per serving)

### Ingredients
- 2/3 cup quinoa
- 2 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
- 1/2 cup chopped pecans
- 1/4 cup pumpkin seeds
- 2 tablespoons unsweetened shredded coconut
- 1 tablespoon sesame seeds
- 1/4 cup maple syrup
- 2 tablespoons coconut oil, melted
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cardamom
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 large egg white
- 2 tablespoons dried goji berries
- 6 cups plain nonfat Greek yogurt
- Fresh berries or other chopped fruit, optional

### Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 325 F. Line a baking sheet with parchment.
2. In a large bowl, combine the quinoa, oats, pecans, pumpkin seeds, coconut, and sesame seeds.
3. Stir in the maple syrup, coconut oil, vanilla, cinnamon, cardamom, and salt until incorporated.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk the egg white until soft peaks form, about 2 minutes. Gently fold it into the granola.
5. Spread the granola evenly on the baking sheet and bake until it begins to brown. Remove from the oven, stir, return to the oven, and continue baking until toasted and crisp, about another 5 minutes.
6. Transfer the granola to a bowl and stir in the goji berries. Let the granola cool.
7. Portion 1/3 cup of granola into bowls, top each serving with 1/2 cup of yogurt, and add fresh fruit for garnish if desired.

### 2. Creamy Tortilla Soup
![Creamy Tortilla Soup](assets/recipes/imported/creamy-tortilla-soup.png)

**Recipe Owner:**  
**Source:** Noom cookbook (`IMG_5286` and `IMG_5287`)  
**Date Made:**  
**Rating:**  
**Would Make Again:**  
**Prep time:** 15 min  
**Cook time:** 30 min  
**Estimated macros:** ~393 cal | 31.8g protein | 13.8g fat | 40.2g carbs (per serving)

### Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon plus 2 teaspoons olive oil
- 1 small onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 1/2 teaspoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 6 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 14-ounce cans diced tomatoes, with juices, or 3 cups diced ripe tomatoes
- 1 15-ounce can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1/2 cup frozen corn kernels, thawed
- 2 teaspoons finely chopped chipotle chile in adobo sauce
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more for seasoning
- 12 ounces cooked boneless, skinless chicken breast, shredded
- 4 6-inch corn tortillas
- 1 large avocado, halved, pitted, and diced
- 6 tablespoons finely diced red onion
- 6 tablespoons plain nonfat Greek yogurt
- 6 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro

### Instructions
1. Heat 1 tablespoon of the olive oil in a medium pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook, stirring, until lightly golden, 4 to 5 minutes. Add the garlic, chili powder, and cumin and cook, stirring, until the spices are fragrant, 1 to 2 minutes.
2. Add the broth, tomatoes with their juices, black beans, corn, chipotle chile, and 1/2 teaspoon of the salt. Bring to a boil, reduce the heat to medium-low, and simmer uncovered until the broth thickens slightly and deepens in color, 20 to 30 minutes.
3. While the soup is cooking, preheat the oven to 375 F.
4. Brush both sides of the tortillas with the remaining 2 teaspoons olive oil, then season with the remaining 1/4 teaspoon salt. Stack the tortillas and use a sharp knife or pizza cutter to cut them into 1/4-inch-thick strips. Arrange the strips on a rimmed baking sheet and bake until crisp and lightly golden, about 15 minutes.
5. Stir the chicken into the soup and heat until warmed through, 3 to 4 minutes.
6. Taste the soup and season with additional salt if needed.
7. Divide the soup among 6 bowls. Top each bowl with tortilla strips, avocado cubes, red onion, yogurt, and cilantro.

### Notes/Changes
- Serves 6.

### 3. Garlicky Dill Tzatziki
**Recipe Owner:**  
**Source:** Adapted from [Suzy Karadsheh / The Mediterranean Dish](https://www.themediterraneandish.com/tzatziki-sauce-recipe/), with a measured dill addition and adjustable garlic.  
**Date Made:**  
**Rating:**  
**Would Make Again:**  
**Prep time:** 15 min, plus 30 min–2 hours chilling  
**Cook time:** None  
**Servings:** About 8 generous 1/4-cup portions  
**Estimated macros:** ~75 cal | 5g protein | 5g fat | 3g carbs (per portion; rough estimate with 5% Greek yogurt)

### Ingredients
- 3/4 large English cucumber
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt, divided; adjust to taste
- 2 cups plain full-fat Greek yogurt
- 4 garlic cloves, finely grated (start with 2 for milder sauce)
- 1 teaspoon white vinegar
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil, plus optional finishing drizzle
- 2 tablespoons finely chopped fresh dill
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

### Instructions
1. Grate cucumber and toss with 1/2 teaspoon salt. Wrap in a clean kitchen towel and wring very thoroughly; discard liquid.
2. Stir garlic, vinegar, oil, and remaining salt together. Fold in yogurt, squeezed cucumber, dill, and pepper.
3. Cover and refrigerate 30 minutes to 2 hours. Stir, taste, and adjust seasoning before serving. Finish with an optional little olive oil.

### Notes/Changes
- Untested in this kitchen. Aim for a thick sauce that stays on a spoon; draining the cucumber and using strained yogurt make the difference.
- Garlic intensifies as it rests. Start with 2 cloves if making ahead, then decide whether to add more after chilling.
- Refrigerate airtight and use within 2–4 days.
- Serve with [Homemade Pizza Oven Pita](recipes/to-try/pizza-oven-pita.md) and [Viral Döner Kebab](recipes/to-try/viral-doner-kebab.md).
- Dinner timing: mix pita dough first, make and chill the sauce during its rise, then bake the meat and cook pita close to serving.

### 4. Homemade Pita (Pizza Oven)
**Recipe Owner:**  
**Source:** Dough adapted from [King Arthur Baking / Golden Pita Bread](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/golden-pita-bread-recipe); pizza-oven adjustments below are untested kitchen guidance.  
**Date Made:**  
**Rating:**  
**Would Make Again:**  
**Prep time:** 25 min active; about 1 hour 15 min rising/resting  
**Cook time:** Approximately 3–4 min per pita; varies with oven  
**Servings:** 8 pitas  
**Estimated macros:** ~200 cal | 5g protein | 4g fat | 36g carbs (per pita; calculated roughly from ingredients)

### Ingredients
- 360g all-purpose flour (3 cups), plus dusting flour
- 227g lukewarm water (1 cup)
- 2 teaspoons instant yeast
- 2 teaspoons sugar
- 9g table salt (1 1/2 teaspoons)
- 25g olive oil (about 2 tablespoons)

### Instructions
1. Mix all ingredients into a shaggy dough. Knead until smooth, about 10 minutes by hand or 5 minutes in a mixer. Cover; rise about 1 hour, until puffy.
2. Divide into eight balls, about 80g each. Cover and rest 15 minutes. Roll on a lightly floured counter into even 7-inch circles, about 1/8 inch thick. Avoid tears.
3. Preheat the pizza oven thoroughly. For this adaptation, start with a stone surface around 500–550°F (260–290°C), measured with an infrared thermometer. Use a low flame if adjustable; position bread away from direct flame.
4. Launch one pita directly onto the stone with a lightly floured peel. Cook until inflated and set, roughly 3–4 minutes at the lower temperature; inspect early and rotate as needed. Remove before it becomes crisp. Wrap the stack in a clean towel.

### Notes/Changes
- Untested in this kitchen. Oven model is unspecified; record stone temperature, flame setting, and actual bake time after the first batch.
- Use the first pita to tune heat: if it scorches before puffing, lower the flame or let the stone cool. If pale and slow to puff, allow the stone to recover heat. A pizza-hot 800–900°F stone is too aggressive for this starting method.
- Keep waiting dough covered. If it springs back while rolling, rest another 5–10 minutes. Use minimal dusting flour and brush excess off the peel to reduce scorching. Launch without parchment.
- Cool before storing airtight; keep up to 2 days at room temperature or freeze.
- Serve with [Viral Döner Kebab](recipes/to-try/viral-doner-kebab.md) and [Garlicky Dill Tzatziki](recipes/to-try/garlicky-dill-tzatziki.md).

### 5. Mediterranean Salad
![Mediterranean Salad](assets/recipes/imported/mediterranean-salad.png)

**Recipe Owner:**  
**Source:** Noom cookbook (`IMG_5283`, `IMG_5284`, and `IMG_5307`)  
**Date Made:**  
**Rating:**  
**Would Make Again:**  
**Prep time:** 15 min, plus marinating  
**Cook time:** 0 min  
**Estimated macros:** ~136 cal | 4.4g protein | 5.5g fat | 18g carbs (per 1 cup)

### Ingredients
- For the Dijon mustard dressing:
- 2 tablespoons minced shallot
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 3 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 2 teaspoons Dijon mustard
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh oregano
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- For the salad:
- 6 vine-ripened small tomatoes, about 2 pounds, each cut into 8 wedges
- 1 cup canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1/2 cup thinly sliced red onion
- 1 ounce sun-dried tomatoes, not oil-packed, thinly sliced
- 2 tablespoons capers, drained and rinsed
- 1/2 cup chopped fresh cilantro

### Instructions
1. In a small jar with a tight-fitting lid, combine the shallot, olive oil, vinegar, mustard, oregano, salt, and pepper, and shake until emulsified.
2. Add the dressing, tomatoes, chickpeas, red onion, sun-dried tomatoes, and capers to a bowl and gently toss to coat. Refrigerate to marinate for at least 1 hour and up to 24 hours.
3. Toss the salad with the cilantro. Divide among 6 bowls and serve the salad chilled or at room temperature.

### 6. Roasted Tomato Cups with Bread Crumbs
![Roasted Tomato Cups with Bread Crumbs](assets/recipes/imported/roasted-tomato-cups-with-bread-crumbs.png)

**Recipe Owner:**  
**Source:** Noom cookbook (`IMG_5279` and `IMG_5280`)  
**Date Made:**  
**Rating:**  
**Would Make Again:**  
**Prep time:** 5 min  
**Cook time:** 15 min  
**Estimated macros:** ~76 cal | 2.9g protein | 3.9g fat | 7g carbs (per serving)

### Ingredients
- 1/4 cup panko or other bread crumbs
- 1/4 cup very finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley, plus more for garnish
- 1 teaspoon oil, or oil from anchovy can, for extra flavor
- 1 teaspoon finely grated lemon zest
- 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 6 vine-ripened medium tomatoes, about 1 1/4 pounds, cored and halved crosswise

### Instructions

4. Transfer the tomato halves to a large serving platter or to 6 individual plates and serve hot or at room temperature.

### 7. Tofu & Mango Summer Rolls
![Tofu & Mango Summer Rolls](assets/recipes/imported/tofu-and-mango-summer-rolls.png)

**Recipe Owner:**  
**Source:** Noom cookbook (`IMG_5281` and `IMG_5282`)  
**Date Made:**  
**Rating:**  
**Would Make Again:**  
**Prep time:** 45 min  
**Cook time:** 15 min  
**Estimated macros:** ~160 cal | 6.6g protein | 2.8g fat | 30.8g carbs (per roll, plus 2 tablespoons dipping sauce)

### Ingredients
- For the dipping sauce:
- 2 small limes
- 1 tablespoon fish sauce
- 1 tablespoon water
- 2 teaspoons light brown sugar or palm sugar
- 2 teaspoons unseasoned rice wine vinegar
- 1 teaspoon sliced Thai bird's-eye chili, or any small hot chile
- 1 garlic clove, very finely minced
- For the rolls:
- 1/2 14-ounce package extra-firm tofu
- 2 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
- 8 8-inch round rice wrappers
- 16 large fresh basil leaves
- 2 small radishes, very thinly sliced
- 4 to 6 red-leaf lettuce leaves, rinsed and patted dry, center stems removed, leafy parts cut into 3-by-1-inch rectangles
- 2 small carrots, julienned into 3-inch strips
- 2 small Persian cucumbers, peeled and julienned into 3-inch strips
- 1/2 firm, ripe mango, peeled and julienned into 3-inch strips
- 16 to 24 small fresh mint leaves

### Instructions
1. Finely zest the limes into a medium bowl and set the zest aside. Halve and juice the limes, about 1/4 cup juice, into a small bowl. Add the fish sauce, water, brown sugar, vinegar, chili, and garlic and stir to dissolve the sugar, about 30 seconds.
2. Pat the tofu dry, then cut it into 1/4-inch slices, 4 or 5 slices.
3. Preheat a large 12-inch, dry, no-oil-spray nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Place the tofu slices in the pan and press down on them for 10 seconds, then let them fry for about 3 minutes; the tofu will begin to release water.
4. After about 4 minutes, the undersides will be browned and crisp. Flip the tofu and repeat the process until both sides are browned and crisp, another 3 to 4 minutes.
5. Add the strips to the bowl of lime zest and toss to coat. Sprinkle with the soy sauce and toss to coat again. Wash and dry the skillet.
6. Assemble the summer rolls. For photos and assembly cues, the source page refers to page 27.
7. Place 2 rolls each on 4 individual plates and serve immediately with the dipping sauce.

### Notes/Changes
- Serves 4.
- The source page includes full ingredient amounts and cooking steps, but the visual assembly walkthrough is referenced to another page in the cookbook.

### 8. Ultimate Vegetable Soup
![Ultimate Vegetable Soup](assets/recipes/imported/ultimate-vegetable-soup.png)

**Recipe Owner:**  
**Source:** Noom cookbook (`IMG_5288` and `IMG_5289`)  
**Date Made:**  
**Rating:**  
**Would Make Again:**  
**Prep time:** 20 min  
**Cook time:** 55 min  
**Estimated macros:** ~138 cal | 5.2g protein | 4.5g fat | 20.5g carbs (per serving)

### Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 pound white button mushrooms, trimmed and thinly sliced
- 2 medium zucchini, diced
- 2 large carrots, diced
- 1 large celery stalk, cut into thin slices
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more for seasoning
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper, plus more for seasoning
- 6 cups low-sodium vegetable broth
- 1 28-ounce can diced tomatoes
- 1 15-ounce can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 Parmesan rind, optional
- 4 cups raw baby spinach leaves, stems removed and leaves coarsely chopped

### Instructions
1. Heat the olive oil in a large 6-quart pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook, stirring, until lightly golden, 8 to 9 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Add the mushrooms and cook, stirring, until they release their water and soften, 5 to 6 minutes.
2. Add the zucchini, carrots, celery, salt, oregano, thyme, and pepper and cook, stirring, until the vegetables begin to soften, about 5 minutes.
3. Add the vegetable broth, tomatoes with their juices, chickpeas, bay leaf, and Parmesan rind if using. Bring to a boil, reduce the heat to medium-low, and simmer partially covered, stirring occasionally, until the soup thickens slightly, about 45 minutes. Stir in the spinach and cook an additional 5 minutes.
4. Remove the Parmesan rind and bay leaf, then season to taste with additional salt and pepper. Divide among 8 bowls and serve.

### 9. Viral Döner Kebab (Parchment Roll Method)
**Recipe Owner:**  
**Source:** Adapted from [Yumna Jawad / Feel Good Foodie](https://feelgoodfoodie.net/recipe/doner-kebab/)  
**Date Made:**  
**Rating:**  
**Would Make Again:**  
**Prep time:** 15 min  
**Cook time:** About 18–25 min, plus a brief cooling period  
**Servings:** 8  
**Estimated macros:** ~263 cal | 22g protein | 18g fat | 3g carbs (per serving; source estimate, excludes bread and toppings)

### Ingredients
- 2 pounds ground beef, 85% lean
- 1 large onion, grated
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/4 cup whole-milk Greek yogurt
- 2 teaspoons each ground cumin and ground coriander
- 1 teaspoon each paprika, dried oregano, and salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 red onion and 2 Roma tomatoes, cut into wedges
- Optional serving extras: warm pita, lettuce, pickles, and tahini sauce

### Instructions
1. Heat oven to 400°F (205°C). Combine beef, grated onion, garlic, yogurt, and seasonings thoroughly until cohesive. Divide into four portions.
2. Sandwich each portion between two 12 × 16-inch parchment sheets. Roll into a thin, even rectangle. Peel off the upper sheet.
3. Roll from a long edge, winding the bottom parchment together with the meat into a compact, flattened roll. Repeat for all four portions.
4. Arrange rolls on a rimmed baking sheet with the vegetable wedges alongside. Bake for about 15 minutes, continuing as needed until the beef reaches 160°F (71°C) in the center.
5. Cool briefly, unwrap, and tear into strips. Discard all parchment. Spread meat on the bare pan and broil 2–3 minutes, watching closely. Serve with the vegetables and optional extras.

### Notes/Changes
- Make it a meal with [Homemade Pizza Oven Pita](recipes/to-try/pizza-oven-pita.md) and [Garlicky Dill Tzatziki](recipes/to-try/garlicky-dill-tzatziki.md).
- Untested in this kitchen; record timing, texture, and rating after the first batch.
- Use oven-safe parchment rated for 400°F or higher, never wax paper. Remove every piece of parchment before broiling.
- The parchment winds between the thin layers of meat; it is more than an outside wrapper. Open carefully to avoid escaping steam and hot juices.
- Measure doneness with a thermometer; roll thickness and oven performance affect baking time.

### 10. White Bean & Sausage Soup
![White Bean & Sausage Soup](assets/recipes/imported/white-bean-and-sausage-soup.png)

**Recipe Owner:**  
**Source:** Noom cookbook (`IMG_5290` and `IMG_5291`)  
**Date Made:**  
**Rating:**  
**Would Make Again:**  
**Prep time:** 20 min  
**Cook time:** 40 min  
**Estimated macros:** ~152 cal | 5.4g protein | 5g fat | 16.6g carbs (per 1 1/2 cups)

### Ingredients
- 12 ounces Swiss chard
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 8 ounces hot or sweet Italian-flavored turkey sausage, crumbled
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 6 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 14-ounce can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 14-ounce can diced tomatoes
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon dried basil

### Instructions
1. Separate the chard leaves from the stems. Trim the tough ends from the bottoms of the stems, then thinly slice the stems. Roll the chard leaves into a log, then slice them into 1/2-inch-thick ribbons.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large 6-quart pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
3. Add the sausage and cook, stirring and breaking up any large pieces, until evenly browned, 5 to 6 minutes. Transfer to a bowl to cool.
4. Add the onion and chard stems to the pot and cook over medium-high heat, stirring up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot, until lightly golden, 8 to 9 minutes. Add the garlic and cook, stirring, 1 more minute. Add the chard greens and cook, stirring, until they are wilted but still bright green, about 3 minutes.
5. Return the sausage to the pot, then add the broth, beans, tomatoes and their juice, salt, oregano, and basil. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then reduce to medium-low and simmer, stirring occasionally, until the soup thickens slightly, 25 to 30 minutes.
6. Ladle the soup into 8 individual bowls and serve.

- Serves 8.

### 11. Crispy Spiced Oven-Baked Chickpeas
![Crispy Spiced Oven-Baked Chickpeas](assets/recipes/imported/crispy-spiced-oven-baked-chickpeas.png)

**Recipe Owner:**  
**Source:** Noom cookbook (`IMG_5277` and `IMG_5278`)  
**Date Made:** 4/8/26  
**Rating:** 3 stars  
**Would Make Again:** No  
**Prep time:** 15 min  
**Cook time:** 35 min  
**Estimated macros:** ~217 cal | 7.4g protein | 6.3g fat | 33.7g carbs (per 1/2 cup)

### Ingredients
- 2 14- to 16-ounce cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt
- 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 1/2 teaspoons sweet paprika
- 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cumin

### Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 425 F.
2. Line a rimmed baking sheet with a clean kitchen towel or 2 layers of paper towels and arrange the chickpeas on the towel. Place another towel on top of the chickpeas and gently shake the baking sheet to absorb any additional moisture; the drier the chickpeas are, the crispier they will get. Remove and discard any errant chickpea skins, then transfer the chickpeas to a large bowl. Remove the towel from the baking sheet.
3. Add the olive oil, salt, smoked and sweet paprikas, and cumin to the chickpeas. Stir to coat, then spread them evenly on the baking sheet. Roast the chickpeas, shaking the sheet occasionally to move them around, until they are golden and crisp, 30 to 35 minutes or longer if necessary.
4. Allow the chickpeas to cool completely. Store them in an airtight container for up to 4 days.

### 12. Sweet Potato Chips with Onion Dip
![Sweet Potato Chips with Onion Dip](assets/recipes/imported/sweet-potato-chips-with-onion-dip.png)

**Recipe Owner:**  
**Source:** Noom cookbook (`IMG_5275`, `IMG_5276`, and `IMG_5304`)  
**Date Made:** 4/9/26  
**Rating:** 3 out of 5 stars  
**Would Make Again:** No  
**Prep time:** 25 min  
**Cook time:** 1 hr 20 min  
**Estimated macros:** ~199 cal | 5.6g protein | 10.9g fat | 20.5g carbs (1/2 cup chips and 1/4 cup dip)

### Ingredients
- For the dip:
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 very large onion, finely minced, about 2 cups
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
- 2 tablespoons light mayonnaise
- 3 tablespoons finely minced fresh chives
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- For the chips:
- Olive oil cooking spray
- 2 medium sweet potatoes, about 1 pound, scrubbed and dried, ends trimmed, peel left on
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

### Instructions
1. Cook the onion: Heat the olive oil in a large 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat. Add the onion and 1/4 teaspoon of the salt and cook, stirring, for 5 minutes. Reduce the heat to medium-low, add the garlic, and cook, stirring, until the onion is golden but not charred, about 10 more minutes.
2. Transfer the onion mixture to a medium bowl and let it cool for at least 10 minutes.
3. Make the dip: When the onion is no longer hot, add the yogurt, mayonnaise, chives, garlic and onion powder, remaining 1/4 teaspoon salt, and the pepper and stir to combine. Chill for at least 1 hour to let the flavors meld. The dip can be made up to 3 days ahead; keep covered and chilled.
4. Make the chips: Preheat the oven to 375 F. Place a metal roasting rack on a rimmed baking sheet. Spray the rack with olive oil cooking spray.
5. Slice the potatoes on a mandoline into thin slices about 1/16 inch. If using a knife instead, cut the slices as thin as possible.
6. Pat the potatoes dry between 2 layers of paper towels, then place them in a large bowl, add the olive oil, salt, and pepper, and toss, making sure the pieces are not stuck to each other.
7. Separate the sweet potato slices and lay half of them on the wire rack, avoiding any overlap. Bake until golden and crisp, rotating the pan from front to back halfway through the cooking time, 25 to 30 minutes total.
8. Allow the chips to cool, then transfer to a second baking sheet or large platter. They will continue to crisp as they cool.
9. Repeat with the remaining potato slices, making sure they also bake until crisp as well.
10. Place servings of the chips in individual snack cups and serve along with the dip, or place the chips in a large bowl and serve family style.

### Notes/Changes
- Total time on the source page is 1 hour 45 minutes and serves 6.
- Not a keeper.
